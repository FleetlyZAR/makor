# Photoreal treatment: proposal (7 October 2026)

A test of moving Makor films from painted matte paintings to photoreal cinema,
tried on one chapter of The Garden ("To cultivate and keep", Genesis 2:15 to 17,
shots s036 to s048). Nothing in `films/STYLE-BIBLE.md` changes until Luyanda
approves; if approved, the sections below replace "The look", "People" and the
two suffixes there.

## The look

- Photorealistic cinema, as if shot on 35 mm film with natural light. Real skin,
  real soil, real plants. Not glossy, not fantasy, no lens flares.
- The Makor palette stays as the colour grade: deep ink teal shadows, warm muted
  gold highlights, warm cream whites. No saturated reds or purples.
- Still and reverent: one subject, calm negative space, slow camera.

## Adam (and the people who follow)

- Adam is cast once and kept: `films/tests/photoreal-adam/stills/s001b.jpg`
  is his reference and goes in the Refs of every shot he is in. Turnaround:
  `s002b.jpg`.
- Appearance: a man of the ancient Near East (the Tigris and Euphrates lands),
  about thirty, dark olive brown skin with a reddish earth undertone (Adam, from
  adamah, the ground), short tight black curls, short dense beard, weathered and
  lean. Not the pale European Adam of Renaissance art. Never the long haired
  screen look that reads as Christ.
- People may now be seen at medium distance and their faces may be seen. No
  extreme close ups of faces; the frame stays at chest up or wider.
- Before the Fall (Genesis 2:25) Adam and the woman are unclothed: framed from
  the chest up, or in profile, with plants, grass or the landscape covering the
  rest. Never nudity below the chest. After 3:21, garments of untailored animal
  hide tied with a leather strip; no woven cloth before then.
- Each new person gets the same treatment: a casting portrait, a turnaround
  sheet, one approval, then the reference in every shot.

## The world

- Plants and animals of the ancient Near East only: date palms, figs,
  pomegranates, olives, vines, reeds, wheat and barley grasses; ibex, gazelle,
  onager, lion, oryx. No tropical plants (the model drifts to mango and palms of
  the wrong kind unless they are named).
- The tree of the knowledge of good and evil is a tree of no recognisable
  species. Never an apple.

## What never changes

- God is never shown, only by effect (light, wind, water, a voice). Never the
  face of Christ. The Spirit is wind. Never invent a scene the text does not
  give. Banned prompt words stay banned.

## Consequences to accept

- YouTube's AI disclosure becomes **Yes** (realistic people who did not exist).
- The Seven Days, The Garden and The Fall are painted. Either they stay as they
  are (the series changes style from the next film), or each is redone at
  about 6 USD of keyframes for The Garden (92 shots at the Batch price of
  0.067 USD, plus rerolls) and any Veo clips that have to be redone.
- Veo with people: the sample's three Adam clips (walking, working the soil,
  sitting under the fig) were made on Veo 3.1 Standard (3.20 USD each) because
  the day's Fast and Lite quotas had gone to the daily tasks. His face held in
  all three. Fast (0.96 USD) is the normal choice; check every clip for face
  drift and for the framing slipping below the chest, and use only the clean
  seconds. Depth motion stays the default for shots without movement in them.
- Safety filter: Nano Banana refuses prompts that say "no clothing" for a
  seated man; describe the framing ("bare shoulders, tall grass in front of
  him") instead.

## Photoreal suffix (proposed)

    Photorealistic cinematic film still, shot on 35mm film, natural light, colour
    grade of deep ink teal shadows and warm muted gold highlights, warm cream
    whites, no saturated reds or purples, calm and reverent, wide 16:9
    composition with calm low detail space in the lower third, full frame with no
    borders or black bars, no text, no lettering, no watermark, not a painting,
    not an illustration, not a 3D render, not cartoon.
