### 1. A world of other stories (0:50 to 2:05)

Picture: the Nile at blazing dawn, kilns and stacked bricks, a clay tablet pressed with wedge
script, a storm over a dark sea; objects and landscapes only (style bible). The brick-maker
seen once more, at work under the sun.
Voice: v1 GUIDE lines m005 to m010, trimmed: Moses and the Torah; Marduk and Tiamat; the sun as
Egypt's god; "Genesis speaks into that world with deliberate restraint. There is no battle";
ends on "...the Maker of the sun that Egypt worshipped, the sea that the nations feared."
Sound: river, kiln roar, wind; a tablet stylus scratch. **Cue 2.**
Exit (open loop): hard cut to black and silence.
