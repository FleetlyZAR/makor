# The Seven Days: production plan for the full movement film

Genesis 1:1 to 2:3, about 15 minutes, for YouTube. Script:
`script/movement-script.md` (generated by `script/movement.py`). Rules, style
and voices: `films/STYLE-BIBLE.md`, `films/VOICES.md`. Method proven on the Day
One pilot (`exports/day-01-v2.mp4`).

## 1. What the pilot taught us (carried into this plan)

- **Lock timing before paying for pictures.** Voices are cheap or free, video is
  not. The full voice track and a rough animatic come first, so every paid clip
  is cut to a known length.
- **Veo dramatises.** Unless told otherwise it adds breaking surf, sun glints
  and early light. Calm water wording and "no source" wording are now in the
  style bible; first and last frame interpolation is the tool for controlled
  transitions (light arriving, evening to morning).
- **Free fixes in the edit beat rerolls.** Trims, slow downs, freeze frames,
  reversed footage and still pushes saved four paid rerolls on Day One.
- **Gold leaks into dark shots** through the style suffix; the "before the
  light" palette clause fixes it.
- **Veo has a daily request cap.** The pilot hit HTTP 429 after 13 requests in
  one day. At that rate the full film's clips take about a week of calendar
  time unless the quota is raised.
- **Captions must sit on a soft ink band** or they vanish on gold and cream.

## 2. Format

| | Full film | Shorts cut from it |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | 16:9, 1920 by 1080 (recommended) | 9:16, 1080 by 1920 |
| Length | about 15 minutes | 30 to 60 seconds each |
| Voices | READER, GOD, GUIDE | Scripture only shorts: READER and GOD. The one minute movement short: GUIDE led |
| Captions | Scripture burned in as a lower third on the ink band; full SRT uploaded as YouTube captions | Burned in, as in the pilot |

The pilot is 9:16, so its shots cannot drop straight into a 16:9 film. Its
prompts and keyframes are reused as references, regenerated at 16:9.

**Getting 9:16 shorts from a 16:9 film.** A 9:16 crop of 1080p is only 607
pixels wide, too soft. Options:

- **A. Separate 9:16 passes for the shorts (recommended).** Each day's short
  regenerates its 6 to 8 key shots at 9:16 using the film's 16:9 keyframes as
  reference. About 7 dollars a short.
- **B. Shoot the film's Scripture shots in 4K** (Veo Fast 4K, 0.30 per second)
  and crop 9:16 from 3840 by 2160. Simpler pipeline, roughly double the video
  cost.

## 5. Milestones (each ends at a checkpoint)

**M0. Decisions.** Format, budget cap, Veo quota (see section 7).

**M1. Script lock and voice track.** You read the script and mark any GUIDE
wording to change. Then render every line: READER and GOD in ElevenLabs
(`eleven_multilingual_v2`), GUIDE in Kokoro `am_michael`. Real durations
replace the estimates. Cost: about 1 dollar of credits.

**M2. Animatic.** The whole film assembled from the voice track, temp music and
plain title cards for each shot (free). You watch 15 minutes of timing and
structure before any picture money is spent. This is where pacing gets fixed.

**M3. World bible and look development.** New things appear after Day One that
the pilot never tested: land, plants, sun and moon, sea creatures, birds,
animals, people, Egypt and Babylon objects, the empty tomb, the new creation.
One **hero geography** (a bay with hills and a river mouth) is designed once and
seen again on Days Three to Seven so the world visibly fills rather than
changing place. About 10 test stills. Output: an updated style bible with a
reference still for each element.

**M4. Shot list and keyframes, chapter by chapter.** A generator turns the
script beats into a shot list (shot, duration, picture, motion, prompt with the
right suffix variant). Keyframes go in batches of one or two chapters, each
reviewed as a contact sheet. Earlier keyframes are passed as reference images
for continuity (to confirm in the Nano Banana Pro docs before use).

**M5. Motion.** Veo clips only for the shots marked Veo, in daily batches
within the quota, each reviewed as a frame strip; still pushes for the rest are
built in the edit for free.

**M6. Sound and music.** Sound effect list per chapter (water, wind, the land
rising, birds, whales, herds, the stillness of Day Seven). Music as four or five
Suno tracks matched to the acts: the dark and the word (cold open to Day One),
forming (Days Two and Three), filling (Days Four to Six), rest (Day Seven), and
Christ and new creation (to the end).

**M7. Edit.** The Day One build script grows into a timeline driven editor:
one JSON timeline for the whole film (shots, cues, captions), 16:9, lower third
Scripture captions on the ink band, SRT export, music ducked under speech,
loudness to -14 LUFS, chapter markers checked against the timeline.

**M8. Review and publish pack.** Frame by frame rules review, every verse
checked against the JSON, runtime, total spend; YouTube title, description with
chapters and study link first, thumbnail options, pinned comment. No upload.

## 6. Tooling to build

- `script/movement.py` already verifies Scripture; extend it to emit the shot
  list and the voice line list.
- `films/tools/still.py` and `clip.py`: add an aspect ratio flag (16:9) and
  reference images; read prompts from the shot list.
- `films/tools/voice.py` and `narrate.py`: read the movement line list.
- `edit/` timeline builder, generalising `build.py`.

## 7. Decisions (agreed 5 October 2026)

1. Format: 16:9 1920 by 1080 film; shorts as separate 9:16 passes using the
   film's keyframes as reference (option A).
2. Budget cap for the full film: 150 US dollars, logged in
   `COSTS-movement.md`, checkpoint at every 25 dollars.
3. Veo quota: Luyanda checks https://ai.dev/rate-limit and requests a higher
   limit if offered; otherwise clips run in daily batches.
4. Captions: Scripture burned in as a lower third, plus a full SRT for
   YouTube closed captions.
