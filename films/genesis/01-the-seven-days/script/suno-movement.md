# The Seven Days: Suno prompts for the full film score

Six instrumental tracks, one per act, all in the same family as the Day One
track "Light Breaking" so the film sounds like one score. Times come from the
rendered voice track (`script/movement-script.md`). Suno rarely lands exact
lengths; aim for the length shown or a little longer and I will cut, loop and
crossfade in the edit.

Drop each finished WAV into `films/genesis/01-the-seven-days/audio/music/` named
`act1.wav` to `act6.wav` (or tell me the file names). WAV is the format I prefer.

For every track:

- Mode: Custom. Instrumental: on.
- If your Suno has "Cover" or "Use as inspiration", give it "Light Breaking" as
  the reference so the instruments and room stay the same.
- Exclude styles (if the box exists):

```
drums, percussion, vocals, choir, chanting, epic trailer hits, electronic beat, synth arpeggio, guitar
```

The voice carries the film. The music should leave the middle of the sound open
(no busy melodies in the speech range) and move in long, slow arcs.

---
