# Day One: Suno prompt for the score

Make it in Suno as an instrumental, then drop the file you like into
`films/genesis/01-the-seven-days/audio/music/` (any name, mp3 or wav). Suno
rarely hits exact seconds, so aim for a track of 60 to 90 seconds; I will cut
and fade it to the picture in the edit. If a take has a clear rise and a clear
resolution, that is more important than its exact length.

## Settings

- Mode: Custom
- Instrumental: on (no vocals of any kind)
- Title: Makor Day One

## Style of music (paste into the Style box)

```
sparse cinematic ambient score, solo cello and low strings, soft felt piano, warm analog pad, very slow, reverent, quiet and spacious, gradual rise to a warm major swell, gentle resolution, no drums, no vocals, no choir
```

## Exclude styles (if your Suno version has the box)

```
drums, percussion, vocals, choir, chanting, epic trailer, electronic beat, synth arpeggio, guitar
```

## Lyrics box (structure only, no words are sung)

```
[Instrumental]

[Intro]
[near silence, one low held cello note in darkness, very soft]

[Verse]
[slow and sparse, low strings and felt piano, dark and still, waiting]

[Build]
[gentle rising tension, strings slowly climb]

[Swell]
[warm major chord blooms open, light breaking, full but never loud]

[Bridge]
[calm and ordered, steady warm strings, piano answers]

[Resolution]
[settles onto a resolved major chord, peaceful]

[Outro]
[fades to near silence, one soft unresolved note left hanging, expectant]

[End]
```

## How it will sit in the film

| Film time   | Picture and voice                          | What the music should do                  |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 0 to 6 s    | Black, then dark water; Genesis 1:1         | Near silence, one low held note           |
| 6 to 19 s   | The deep, the wind; Genesis 1:2             | Sparse and dark, barely moving            |
| 19 to 22 s  | Darkness held; GOD: "Let there be light,"   | Pull back almost to nothing under the voice |
| 22 to 27 s  | Light breaks across the deep                | The swell: warm and full, the high point  |
| 27 to 46 s  | Light, day and night, order; verses 4 and 5 | Calm, ordered, warm, under the narration  |
| 46 to 52 s  | Evening and morning, the first day          | Resolves on a major chord                 |
| 52 to 58 s  | Unshaped waters; "But the waters still have no shape." | Near silence, one note left open |

In the edit the music is ducked under every spoken line, and it drops almost
to silence under God's line so the voice stands alone.
