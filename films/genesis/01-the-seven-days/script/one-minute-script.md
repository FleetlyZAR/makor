# The Seven Days in a minute

One GUIDE led short that explains the whole movement, Genesis 1:1 to 2:3, for Instagram
Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and WhatsApp Status. 9:16, 1080x1920, about 58 s (under the
60 s that suits all four; WhatsApp Status allows up to 90 s).

Intention (from the study's thesis): before the drama of a fallen world begins, God speaks a
formless void into an ordered, good and habitable home. One idea only: **God takes a world
that is formless and void and, by His word, shapes it and fills it, and then rests.**

Voices follow films/VOICES.md: READER for Scripture, GOD for God's own words, GUIDE
(`am_michael`) for everything else. Scripture is verbatim BSB from the study JSON and every
verse used is complete. GUIDE lines are drawn from the study (field in brackets).
Timings are measured: the Scripture lines are the film's own renders, the GUIDE lines were
rendered locally to time them.

## How I would make it

1. **Voices**: reuse the film's READER and GOD renders for 1:1, 1:3 and 1:27 (already paid
   for), and render the three GUIDE lines locally with Kokoro. Cost: nothing.
2. **Pictures**: the film's keyframes are 2752x1536, so a 9:16 crop of each (864x1536) only
   needs a gentle 1.25x upscale to 1080x1920, which keeps the painted texture. Each shot
   gets a slow push. Cost: nothing.
3. **Optional polish**: regenerate the four hero moments natively at 9:16 (the deep, the
   light breaking, the figures on the ridge, the bay at rest), using the 16:9 keyframes as
   references so they match exactly: 4 stills, about 0.54 USD; and animate two of them with
   Veo at 9:16 (the light, the rest): about 1.92 USD, within the daily limit.
4. **Music**: the act 1 score for the opening, crossfading into act 4 and resolving with
   act 6 at the new creation; ducked under every line.
5. **One master, four uploads**: one 1080x1920 file with captions kept inside the safe area
   of TikTok and Reels (clear of the bottom 420 px and right 150 px). For WhatsApp Status,
   a lighter 720x1280 copy so it is not crushed by WhatsApp's compression.
6. **Per platform text**: a short caption and hashtags for each (Reels and TikTok: #Genesis
   #Bible #Creation #BibleStudy; Shorts: title "The Seven Days of Creation in 60 Seconds |
   Makor"), each pointing to the full film and the study page.

Total cost: 0 USD as specified, or about 2.50 USD with the native 9:16 hero shots.
