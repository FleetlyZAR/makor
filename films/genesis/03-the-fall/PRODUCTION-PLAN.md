## Decisions (approved by Luyanda at C1, 6 October 2026)

1. **D1 The serpent.** An ordinary real snake, small and far off, partly hidden in the long grass
   at the foot of the trees, muted natural colouring inside the palette. Never a monster, never
   a face, no glowing eyes, no fangs, no open mouth, no legs, never shown speaking, and never in
   the tree (the text does not put it there). Its words are heard over wide shots. In 3:14 it
   goes on its belly through dust; in 3:15 it has gone into shadow while a line of gold light
   falls on the ground. No head, heel or foot is ever shown.
2. **D2 Who reads the speech.** The READER reads the serpent, the woman and the man (the Makor
   rule). The woman's version of God's command in 3:3 ("or touch it") is her words, so it stays
   with the READER; the GOD voice speaks only 3:9, 3:11, 3:13a, 3:14 to 19 and 3:22.
3. **D3 3:8 by sound, wind and light only.** A long wave of wind through the treetops and light
   shifting across the ground between the trunks, then the light moving toward the hidden pair.
   Sound: wind in the leaves and the evening garden. No footsteps (they would imply a body),
   no figure, no light shaped like a person. The same applies to every shot under God's lines.
4. **D4 Fig leaves and garments of skin by object.** 3:7: broad fig leaves in the foreground,
   two tiny figures far beyond in shade. 3:21: plain garments of skin resting in warm light at
   the foot of a tree, the two tiny figures far beyond among the trunks, no clothing detail
   readable. No animal shown dying. Fruit is never an apple: "fruit of no particular kind".
5. **D5 3:24.** No figures. The word cherubim never goes into an image prompt (models paint
   winged figures). Shown as a slow turning flame of light standing in the eastern opening
   between the trees, the pair tiny and from behind walking east into dry land. No gate, no
   wall, no blade held by anyone. Veo Fast for this shot.
6. **D6 How 3:15 leads to Christ.** Chapter "The seed of the woman", GUIDE from `study.christ`
   (the promise narrows through Abraham and David to one person; the last Adam tested in the
   wilderness; Adam and Christ in contrast; the heel wound, Hebrews 2:14), then "The way to the
   tree of life" from `study.typology[3]` and the Revelation 22 cross reference. Pictures by
   setting only: an empty wilderness, an olive grove at night, a garden tomb with the stone
   rolled back, a curtain torn from top to bottom, the river city with the tree of life. No
   cross, no crown of thorns, no figure.
7. **D7 Music.** Recommendation: reuse The Seven Days score again (free, keeps the series one
   sound), mapped by act below, with offsets so the light theme of act6 enters on 3:15. Risk:
   a creation score may feel too warm under 3:6 to 3:19. After M1 I make a free score preview
   against the voice track; if it is too warm, I write two Suno prompts (a darker, sparer act for
   the fall and the questions, and one for the exile) for you to generate.
8. **D8 Act structure** (the study's five scenes, plus Christ):

   | Act | Chapters | Score (Seven Days) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 The deception | Cold open, Did God really say?, She took and ate | act1 (dark and still), faded before its bloom |
   | 2 God seeks | Where are you?, The woman whom You gave me | act5 (very sparse, long silences) |
   | 3 The promise | He will crush your head | act6 from its start (single point of light theme on 3:15) |
   | 4 The sentences | Dust you are | act1 again, from its dark middle |
   | 5 Grace in the exile | Mother of all the living, East of Eden | act4 (stillness, then a tender theme on 3:21) |
   | 6 The seed of the woman | The seed of the woman, The way to the tree of life | act6 from its swell, hope on Revelation 22 |

9. **D9 Quotations of other Scripture.** As in The Garden, seven short phrases verbatim from the
   study, read by the READER (Revelation 12:9, Isaiah 65:25, Galatians 4:4, Matthew 26:39,
   Hebrews 2:14, Hebrews 10:19 to 20, Revelation 22:4). The study holds only phrases of these, not
   whole verses. Every verse of Genesis 3 is read complete. If "every verse complete" should
   also cover these, the alternative is to drop them (the GUIDE refers to the passage by name).
10. **D10 Cold open.** Open on The Garden's own last keyframes (free) so the films join, then a
    movement in the grass.

## Picture rules for this film (on top of the style bible)

- People only from 3:1 as tiny distant figures, behind or in profile, framed by trees and light.
  Strictest in 3:7, 3:10 and 3:21: figures withdrawn into shade, nothing readable.
- No figure in the sky or in the light; no footsteps in the sound.
- Never: an apple, a snake with a face or legs, a winged figure, a gate or wall at Eden,
  buildings, dead trees inside the garden (thorns and thistles only outside it, in the field).
- Banned prompt words as in the style bible; also keep "cherubim" and "serpent" out of image
  prompts (use "a small snake").
