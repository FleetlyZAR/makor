# The Fall: Suno prompts for the score

Method and series sound: `films/SCORING.md`. Seven cues, one per act, replacing the borrowed
Seven Days tracks. Times are from the current voice track (11:24) and may move a few seconds
when breaths are added; aim for the length shown or a little longer and I cut to fit.

Drop the WAVs into `films/genesis/03-the-fall/audio/music/` as `fall-1.wav` to `fall-7.wav`.

For every cue:

- Mode: Custom. Instrumental: on.
- Reference ("Cover" or "Use as inspiration", if your Suno has it): The Seven Days
  `act1.wav`, so the room and instruments match the series.
- Exclude styles:

```
vocals, choir, chanting, epic trailer hits, drum kit, snare, hi-hat, electronic beat, synth arpeggio, guitar, orchestral hits
```

The voice carries the film: leave the speech range open, no busy melodies, long arcs.

## Cue 1: The question (0:00 to 2:14, aim 2:30)

Cold open, Did God really say? The serpent's soft, reasonable questions. Unease under beauty.
Key moment: "You will not surely die" at 1:34; the score falls away under it (done in the mix).

Title: `Makor The Fall 1 The Question`

Style:
```
cinematic underscore, solo cello and low strings, soft felt piano, warm pad, slow heartbeat pulse of low pizzicato at 66 bpm, uneasy and quiet, beautiful but unresolved, a sly minor turn, sparse, no drums, no vocals
```

Lyrics box:
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro]
[a warm garden chord, then a low cello note that does not resolve]
[Verse]
[soft low pizzicato pulse begins, steady, patient, felt piano fragments above]
[Verse]
[pulse continues, harmony slides to minor, quiet unease]
[Bridge]
[strings thin out, only the pulse and a held note]
[Verse]
[pulse returns, a little closer, still quiet]
[Outro]
[hangs on an unresolved chord]
[End]
```

## Cue 2: She took and ate (2:14 to 3:19, aim 1:15)

Good for food, pleasing to the eyes, desirable for wisdom: the climb. Then the eating, and
"the eyes of both of them were opened" at 2:54: everything goes hollow.

Title: `Makor The Fall 2 She Took and Ate`

Style:
```
cinematic underscore, low strings and felt piano over a pulse at 66 bpm that slowly tightens, rising tension in three steps, then sudden collapse to a hollow solo duduk over a thin drone, restrained, no drums, no vocals
```

Lyrics box:
```

[climb a second step, then a third, tighter, never loud]
[Break]
[everything stops at once]
[Outro]
[a lone duduk over a thin low drone, exposed and ashamed]
[End]
```

## Cue 3: Where are you? (3:19 to 5:01, aim 1:50)

The voice of the LORD God walking in the garden; they hide. "Where are you?" at 3:44 is the
centre of the chapter: the score is silent for it and returns softly after. Then the blame.
No pulse in this cue.

Title: `Makor The Fall 3 Where Are You`

Style:
```
very sparse cinematic ambient, wind-like high strings, soft breathy pad, distant duduk, no pulse, still and searching, tender not frightening, long silences, no drums, no vocals
```

## Cue 4: He will crush your head (5:01 to 6:11, aim 1:20)

God sentences the serpent. Then Genesis 3:15 at 5:29: the first promise. This is where the
**promise theme** is born: a short rising line, first on cello, answered by horn.

Title: `Makor The Fall 4 The Promise`

Style:
```
cinematic underscore, low drone and soft frame drum heartbeat at 66 bpm, gravity and judgment, then a short rising major theme on solo cello answered by warm french horn, first light, hopeful but restrained, no choir, no vocals
```

Lyrics box:
```

[Build]
[harmony opens toward major]
[Theme]
[a short rising cello melody, answered by a warm horn, the promise]
[Outro]
[the theme settles on a quiet major chord]
[End]
```

## Cue 7: The seed of the woman (9:19 to 11:24, aim 2:20)

The promise narrows to one person; Christ, the last Adam; the veil torn; the tree of life open
again. **The promise theme returns** from cue 4 and grows to its fullest, then resolves to
quiet hope for the closing question.

Title: `Makor The Fall 7 The Seed of the Woman`

Style:
```
cinematic score, the rising cello theme returns and grows, warm strings, french horn, felt piano, steady hopeful pulse at 68 bpm, builds to a full warm swell, then settles to quiet peace, reverent, no choir, no vocals, no drum kit
```

## After the cues land

Acts in `film.json` become `fall-1.wav` (Cold open), `fall-2.wav` (She took and ate),
`fall-3.wav` (Where are you?), `fall-4.wav` (He will crush your head), `fall-5.wav`
(Dust you are), `fall-6.wav` (East of Eden), `fall-7.wav` (The seed of the woman). I set
offsets so the promise theme lands on 3:15 and the cue 7 swell on the veil torn, add the
breaths, apply the mix targets, and send an A/B of the full film before it replaces the cut.
