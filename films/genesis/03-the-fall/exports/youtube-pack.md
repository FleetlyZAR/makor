## Description

    Study The Fall: https://www.makor.co.za/genesis/the-fall/

    Genesis 2 ended with a man and a woman naked and unashamed in a good garden. Genesis 3
    answers the question every reader asks: if the world began like that, why is it like
    this? This film reads Genesis 3:1 to 24 in full and walks through it: the serpent's
    question about what God said, the eating, the shame, the God who comes looking with
    "Where are you?", the blame, the sentences, and in the middle of them the first promise
    of the gospel, the seed of the woman who will crush the serpent's head. Then the
    garments of skin, the exile east of Eden, and the way back to the tree of life opened in
    Christ.

    Made with AI tools; Scripture from the Berean Standard Bible.
