# The Seven Days v2: cast (photoreal)

The brick-maker (decision D1, 7 October 2026): a Hebrew slave making mud bricks in Egypt
(Exodus 1:13 to 14), the first hearer of Genesis 1, and the human strand of the film. He is
of Abraham's descendants, a Semitic man of the Levant living in Egypt, not of Adam's look.
Three candidates for Luyanda to choose from; the chosen one is saved as
`stills/cast/brickmaker.jpg` and sent as a reference in every shot he appears in.

### Shot 1: brick-maker casting portrait A (young father)
- Image prompt
> Chest up portrait of a Hebrew slave labourer in ancient Egypt, about twenty eight years old, a Semitic man of the ancient Levant: warm olive brown skin darkened by sun, short dark curly hair, a short dark beard, dark brown eyes, a lean strong build from heavy work, dried grey mud smeared on his forearms, shoulders and one cheek, sweat on his skin, a plain undyed rough linen cloth tied over one shoulder. Three quarter view, a tired but steady gaze just past the camera, quiet dignity. Behind him, softly out of focus, the mud brick pits of Egypt at dusk: rows of drying mud bricks and the orange glow of a kiln fire. Photorealistic cinematic film still, shot on 35mm film with a 50mm lens, natural skin texture, warm firelight on one side of his face and cool dusk light on the other, colour grade of deep ink teal shadows and warm muted gold highlights, calm and reverent, no text, no lettering, no watermark, not a painting, not a 3D render, not cartoon.
