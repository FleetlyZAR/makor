# The Seven Days v2: Suno prompts for the signature themes

Epidemic Sound carries most of the score (cue sheet in `../../01-the-seven-days/V2-TREATMENT.md`,
auditions in `music-audition.html`). Suno is for the three moments where Makor should own
its music, themes that can return in later films. Method: `films/SCORING.md` (no reference
audio, genre first, 5 to 8 descriptors, `instrumental` last, the real Exclude field).

## Settings for every theme

- Mode **Custom**, **Instrumental on**, **no reference audio, no Cover, no Persona**.
- Newest model your plan has (v5.5). **Weirdness about 40%, Style Influence about 75%.**
- **Exclude**:

```
vocals, choir, chanting, lyrics, drum kit, snare, hi-hat, electronic beat, synth arpeggio, guitar, dubstep, trailer braams
```

- 4 takes each; keep the one with the clearest, simplest melody (a theme must be hummable).
- Download WAV and, if your plan has it, **Stems > Auto Split**. Save in
  `films/genesis/01-the-seven-days-v2/audio/music-v2/` as `theme-light.wav`,
  `theme-image.wav`, `theme-word.wav` (stems as `theme-light-strings.wav`, and so on).

The film's peaks are allowed to be big: these three are the moments the voice stops and the
music leads (films/CINEMA-STANDARD.md). Epic, but warm and reverent, never aggressive.

---

## Theme 1: Let there be light (set piece, 2:05 to 3:35; also the cold open)

Black water, total darkness, the Spirit as wind over the deep; then "Let there be light" and
the horizon ignites. This is **the Makor theme**: a five or six note rising melody that will
return wherever God makes light (The Word, Easter morning, the new creation).

Title: `Makor Theme 1 Let There Be Light`

Style:
```
epic cinematic orchestral, slow 70 BPM, deep sub drone and low strings in darkness, a simple rising horn theme, full strings and timpani swell, overwhelming warm brightness, awe and wonder, instrumental
```

Lyrics box:
```
[Intro: near silence, a deep low drone, darkness over water]
[Interlude: soft low strings breathe in and out, waiting]
[Break: one second of silence]
[Build: a single horn plays a simple rising theme of five notes, strings rise beneath it]
[Climax: the full orchestra bursts open, timpani, soaring strings, the horn theme in full, blinding warm light]
[Outro: the theme settles on a long bright major chord, then calm]
[End]
```

## Theme 2: In His image (set piece, 9:20 to 12:00)

Herds thunder across the plains; the music cuts to nothing; "Let Us make man in Our image";
dawn; the man and the woman at first light; then the brick-maker's face. The human theme:
dignity given to slaves. Warmer and more intimate than Theme 1 at first, then immense.

Title: `Makor Theme 2 In His Image`

Style:
```
emotional cinematic score, 66 BPM, solo cello melody, soft felt piano, warm strings and french horns, builds from intimate to immense, dignity and tenderness, hopeful, instrumental
```

Lyrics box:
```
[Intro: silence, then one soft piano note like a first breath]
[Verse: a solo cello sings a tender melody, intimate and human]
[Build: warm strings join beneath the cello, the melody climbs]
[Climax: horns and full strings take the melody, immense and warm, dignity and joy]
[Outro: back to solo cello and piano, gentle and full of hope]
[End]
```

## Theme 3: The Word (set piece, 13:15 to 14:25)

A single point of light in darkness widens into dawn; the stone rolled back from the empty
tomb on the first day of the week. Theme 1 returns, transformed: the light theme heard as
resurrection. If Theme 1 lands a melody you love and your plan has Suno Studio, make this by
**Extend** or **Replace Section** from that take so the melody is the same.

Title: `Makor Theme 3 The Word`

Style:
```
reverent cinematic orchestral, slow 68 BPM, solo piano and high strings in stillness, the rising horn theme returns, gradual build to a radiant full orchestra, resurrection morning, hope and glory, instrumental
```

Lyrics box:
```
[Intro: a single high piano note in darkness, very still]
[Verse: soft high strings, a point of light slowly widening]
[Build: the rising horn theme returns, quietly, then stronger]
[Climax: radiant full orchestra, the theme in full glory, first light on the first day]
[Outro: a warm resolved chord held long, peace]
[End]
```
