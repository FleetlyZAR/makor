# The Seven Days (v2): the questions it answers, and a Short for each

Each Short is 40 to 60 seconds, 9:16, built from the film's own footage and, wherever possible, its own recorded
lines (ids such as m020 are lines already voiced in `audio/movement/`, timed in `script/movement-timeline.json`).
Lines marked **NEW** are new GUIDE lines, worded from the study (`src/content/studies/genesis/01-the-seven-days.json`,
the section named in brackets) and would be voiced with the same GUIDE voice (Kokoro am_michael at speed 0.85).
Scripture is quoted exactly from the study (BSB). Shots are the film's (`script/shotlist.json`).

Every Short follows the same frame: a question as the hook in the first second, on screen and spoken; the answer
in the film's own words and pictures; one line that lands it; an end card to the full film. The words are on
screen for silent viewing, as in `edit/short_v2.py`.

## Making them

`edit/short_v2.py` already does the hard parts (vertical reframing by shot, words on screen, the end card). The next
step is a general builder that takes one of the tables above as data: cut the listed shots (re-timed to the new
voice order), lay the listed lines from `audio/movement/` end to end with short gaps, voice the **NEW** lines with
the GUIDE voice, add the score under the voice (a quiet bed from the film's own cues, louder at the start and the
end) and render. Each Short would then take a few minutes, with no new pictures needed.
