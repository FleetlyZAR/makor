## What the scoring taught (apply to the unscored eight)

- **#shorts helps, a little.** Tested three times on the same title with and without it: 84 to 87, 88 to 90,
  89 to 91. Two to three points each time, so every chosen title keeps it.
- **A plain question with the keyword early scores highest** (94 to 96): "Why Does Genesis 1 Have Light Before the
  Sun?", "What Does Made in the Image of God Mean?", "How Long Were the Six Days of Creation?".
- **Statements and curiosity tails score lower** (82 to 89): "Here's Why", "Most People Miss", "the Pattern
  Explained", lists of three options in the title.
- The scorer clusters high and does not check truth, so it is a tie breaker. Where a slightly higher score needed
  a word the Short does not keep (for example "Really" on `days`, 97), the honest version was chosen.
- Every chosen title is under 70 characters including "#shorts". No "| Makor".

## Two current titles to retire (they overclaim)

- `seventh`: "Why the 7th Day of Creation Never Ends" states the long ages reading as fact. The Short only says the seventh
  day has no evening and morning; it never says the day is still running.
- `image`: "Meant to Slaves in Egypt" places the first hearers in Egypt. The Short says they had been treated as tools in
  Pharaoh's building projects; Israel heard Genesis after the exodus. "Mean to Slaves" keeps the point and is true.
