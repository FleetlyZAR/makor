# The Garden v2: clip review (11 October 2026)

110 LTX clips (48 reused v1, The Fall and The Seven Days v2 keyframes, 62 new, plus s003), all rendered
without failures. Every clip was reviewed as a five frame strip (`edit/review/clips-NN.jpg`); the 31
with people were checked at full size (`edit/review/people/`). Machine readable results:
`edit/clip_trims.json`, read by the edit.

## Modesty and continuity (people)

LTX invents clothing and people as figures move: Adam stood up in modern dark trousers (s147), two
extra women walked in beside Eve, one in a modern pink top (s149), and a white garment strip appeared at
Adam's waist as he walked away or toward the camera (s103, s120, s126; s137 in its second half). These
use the approved keyframe with a slow push (or the clean first half). Faces held in every clip: Adam,
Eve, the brick-maker and his wife match their cast portraits from start to end.

Rule for the rest of the series: **LTX on unclothed people only for shots where they stay still**
(lying, sitting, standing in place); any walking or rising goes to depth motion on the keyframe.
