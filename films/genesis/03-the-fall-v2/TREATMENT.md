# The Fall v2: treatment, story arc and storyboard plan

Luyanda, 12 October 2026: "begin the scripting and storyboarding for The Fall. Be as epic as
possible." Built to `films/CINEMA-STANDARD.md` and the method of The Seven Days v2 and The Garden
v2. v1 (`../03-the-fall/`) stays untouched and reproducible; its 98 photoreal keyframes (C2
passed) are the reuse bank for this film.

**Script:** `script/movement.py` (edit that), readable in `script/movement-script.md`. All 24
verses read once, in order, verbatim BSB (the generator checks it). Estimated **18:47** at the v2
voice pace (v1 ran 11:24; The Garden v2 was estimated 15:40 and rendered 15:21). About 1:55 of it
is set pieces and montage with no voice at all.

## The idea

The Fall is the hinge of the whole Bible, so this is the film where the series goes darkest and
biggest. Two ideas carry it:

1. **The Garden played backward.** Genesis 3 runs chapter 2 in reverse, so the film reuses The
   Garden v2's own images and runs them the other way: the eye that opened on the first breath
   opens again in fear; the fall from the sky to a grain of dust becomes a rise from the dust back
   up to the cold sky; the breath is drawn back out. Viewers who watched The Garden will feel it
   before they can name it.
2. **God's question is the centre.** The chapter's concentric shape puts "Where are you?" at its
   heart, so the film opens on it, builds its biggest silence around it, and ends by answering it:
   the brick-maker stepping out of his tent into the light.

The Garden v2 ends on "another voice speaks in the garden, and the first question it asks is
about what God said." The Fall v2 picks up on that exact shot: the tree at dusk, something moving
in the grass.

## The shape

1. **Cold open (flash-forward):** black; a wind walks through the garden tree by tree like
   footsteps; between leaves, Adam's eye, afraid (the shot that opened The Garden, now in fear);
   silence; on black, GOD: "Where are you?" A one second a shot montage: the sword of flame
   igniting, fruit torn away, the serpent's eye, fig leaves, thorns, dust, two figures walking east.
   Then The Garden's last shot, the movement in the grass. Hook: if the world began like that, why
   is it like this? "Another voice asks first." Title.
2. **A crafty voice (3:1):** the serpent close, scales in the grass; the arum and arummim pun. The
   brick-maker's memory of Egypt: the rearing cobra on Pharaoh's brow over the brick yards. Genesis
   refuses to make it a god. Revelation 12:9 and John 8:44 name the voice behind it. He never
   commands; he asks.
3. **Word against word (3:2 to 5):** the dialogue as drama, then the lie built on screen: God's
   words of 2:16 to 17 above hers and the serpent's, with FREELY fading out, OR TOUCH IT added,
   SURELY falling away, NOT dropped in front. The covenant name drops out. Root of sin: distrust.
4. **She took and ate (3:6 to 7), SET PIECE 1, the eating:** extreme slow motion, close; the
   hand, the fruit, the branch springing back; she gives, his hand takes; on the bite the score cuts
   out, the light goes cold, the wind turns (12 s, no voice). Hard cut to the eye opening in fear;
   fig leaves ripped and tied. Then the teaching: appetite, eye, ambition; the tenth commandment;
   the silent man; one act breaks every relationship.
5. **The voice in the garden (3:8 to 10), SET PIECE 2:** God by effect only: one long wave of wind
   through the whole garden, treetop after treetop, light moving between trunks like footsteps,
   the pair pressed among dark trunks; the score builds to a held chord, then everything stops
   (14 s). 3:9 in silence, a 3 s hold, then the man's answer. The camp: wind through the tent
   sanctuary. Walking is the verb of God in Israel's camp; this question is the centre; Cain and
   Elijah; "God's first word to fallen humanity is not a sentence. It is a search."
6. **The woman whom You gave me (3:11 to 13):** the blame as a graphic: serpent to woman to man,
   questions back up, blame back down, sentences in the first order. No question to the serpent.
7. **The long war (3:14 to 15):** the serpent on its belly in the dust; Isaiah 65:25. Dawn light
   across the dust, the serpent rearing at its edge, 3:15, one boom. Enmity is open war; one verb,
   two targets; the seed is he; the first gospel. Then a fast dark **montage of the war** through
   Genesis and Israel (12 s): two altars, a staff dropped in a field, a line of graves, Babel, the
   captives walking east to Babylon, AND THEN HE DIED on screen faster and faster, ending on the
   serpent striking. Cut to black, unresolved.
8. **Dust you are (3:16 to 19), SET PIECE 3, dust to dust:** the woman in the wind; same word for
   pain and toil; description, never charter. Thorns burst from cracked ground in time lapse, the
   man's bleeding hands, sweat on his brow. Then The Garden's opening **played backward**: his eye
   closing, the dust taking him back, one grain, the camera rising up through the clouds into cold
   light (14 s), the breath drawn back out. "And then he died." Mercy laced through judgment.
9. **Clothed (3:20 to 21):** Eve, living, hope in the hour of death; the garments of skin laid on
   their shoulders, fig leaves falling. The brick-maker at the courtyard gate watching Moses clothe
   Aaron's sons in linen tunics, the same word. A life taken (stated as an inference).
10. **East of Eden (3:22 to 24), SET PIECE 4, the biggest in the film:** the tree of life far off;
    exile as mercy. Then they walk east in skins; behind them the whirling sword of flame ignites,
    vast wings of fire and shadow around it (see D3); the camera pulls up and back until they are
    two specks and the flame one point of fire (16 s, choir and drums, the loudest peak). Stationed
    is the verb of God's dwelling. The tabernacle veil with its cherubim; the brick-maker outside,
    looking in. Israel heard its own story in Adam: Hosea 6:7, exile east. East as a direction.
11. **The seed of the woman, SET PIECE 5, the veil to the empty tomb:** the promise narrows; born
    of a woman; the son of Adam; the wilderness temptation over loaf shaped stones, "It is written".
    Gethsemane, a crown of thorns on a stone, a beam against the sky (no figures). The contrasts;
    Hebrews 2:14; the heel and the head in one act. Then darkness, the ground shakes, the veil with
    its cherubim tears from top to bottom, light floods through; dawn at the open tomb; at the foot
    of the rock the serpent lies still in the dust and the wind blows it away as dust (16 s, see D5).
12. **The way back:** the cherubim stand again over the mercy seat, "I will meet with you there";
    the guard at Eden's door becomes the throne of atonement; the new and living way; Revelation 22,
    no more curse, the tree of life open, "will see His face" (8 s swell, the warmest in the film).
13. **Come out (close and open loop):** dawn in the camp, the brick-maker steps out of his dark
    tent into the light and walks to the gate; hear God's questions as grace; the study's question
    on covering shame. Open loop: east of Eden, two altars, two brothers; "the next time God comes
    asking a question, it will be about a brother." End card, WATCH NEXT Cain and Abel.

## Storyboard plan (the set pieces, shot by shot)

The full `script/storyboard.py` comes after the voices and animatic lock the timing, as in The
Garden v2 (cues land on spoken words). The plan below fixes the hero shots now, so casting checks
and the Omni budget can be planned. **R** = reuse an approved v1 Fall keyframe
(`../03-the-fall/stills/movement/`), **G2** = reuse a Garden v2 keyframe or clip, **N** = new
keyframe. Animation per `films/VIDEO-METHODS.md`: LTX locally for most shots, Omni for the takes
marked, and the rule from The Garden v2 that unclothed figures before 3:7 only move in place.

### Set piece 1: the eating (12 s plus 3:6 and 3:7, about 35 s, 12 shots, 2 to 3 s each)

Fruit hanging in warm light (R s022); her eyes, from the brow to the chin (N, Eve); her hand rising
through leaves (N); fingers closing on the fruit (N, Omni slow motion); the branch springing back,
leaves falling (N, Omni); she turns, holding it out, chest up, hair over her shoulders (N, Eve); his
hand takes it (N, Adam's skin tone, bare forearm only); cloud shadow sweeping across the garden (N,
Omni); leaves blowing in a cold wind (R s032); **hard cut** the eye opening in fear (N, mirror of G2
s006); hands tearing fig leaves (R s028 then N); fibre tied (N). All before or at 3:7: faces, hands
and shoulders only, nothing that walks.

### Set piece 2: the voice in the garden (14 s plus 3:8 to 3:10, about 40 s)

One Omni take of 10 s, extended once: the wind entering the garden from far off and travelling
through it in one wave toward the camera, light moving between the trunks (start frame R s031),
cut across six shots of 2 to 3 s; inserts of the pair pressed among the trunks in fig leaves (N,
chest up, LTX in place), their breathing; a 3 s held still on the trunks for 3:9 (R s037).

### The long war montage (12 s, 6 shots of 2 s)

Two altars, one smoke rising, one blown flat (N); a staff lying in a field at dusk (N); a line of
graves along a hillside (N); a brick tower on a plain, people walking east to it (N, distant); a
column of captives walking east toward a walled city (N, distant); the serpent striking (N). Text
AND THEN HE DIED on each, a hit on each. No violence shown.

### Set piece 3: dust to dust (14 s, The Garden v2's open reversed)

The man's eye closing (N, mirror of G2 s006); his face going still on the earth, chest up (N); the
dust drawing in where he lay (reverse of G2 s046 dust shot, made by playing an LTX clip backward);
one grain of dust (G2 s004 clip, reversed); the rise up over bare ground and through the clouds
(The Garden v2's Omni fall s001 to s004, **played in reverse**, about 10 s, free). The breath from
the Garden mix, reversed.

### Set piece 4: east of Eden (16 s plus 3:23 and 3:24, about 45 s)

From inside the garden, the two walking out through the opening in skins, from behind (R s078,
then N with the cast, now clothed so they can walk in LTX); their feet on hard ground (N); the
sword of flame igniting with a roar (R s079, Omni); the wings of flame and shadow around it (N, see
D3); the flame lighting the trunks (N); the pull back and up, one Omni take, from the two figures
to two specks in the dark land with one point of fire behind (start R s080); the garden a dark line
on the horizon (R s083).

### Set piece 5: the veil to the empty tomb (16 s)

Darkness over the land (N); dust shaking on the ground (N); the veil with woven cherubim tearing
from top to bottom, light flooding through (R s094 restaged with cherubim in the weave, Omni); the
torn edge (R s095); dawn at the tomb, stone rolled back (R s090); the light on the path (R s093);
the serpent still in the dust at the foot of the rock, blowing away as dust (N, Omni, see D5); the
same wind through the garden of the tomb (R s092).

### New keyframes this needs

Roughly 60 to 70 new keyframes (cast close ups, the eating, the camp, the war montage, the wings,
the serpent), with 40 to 50 shots reused from v1 and The Garden v2. New world elements: the serpent
close (scales, eye, coils on the branch), the Egyptian brick yard and the carved cobra crown, the
tabernacle courtyard by day with the priests' clothing, the veil inside the holy place, the
wilderness camp at dawn.

## Decisions for Luyanda (as proposed)

- **D1, the human strand.** Continue the brick-maker (cast and turnaround exist in The Garden v2)
  as the first hearer: the cobra on Pharaoh's crown from his slavery in Egypt, wind through the
  camp at 3:8, the priests clothed at 3:21, barred outside the veil at 3:24, stepping out into the
  light at the close. His wife is not needed. Fallback: the camp as places and objects only.
- **D2, Adam and Eve framing.** Before 3:7: faces, hands and shoulders only, nothing that walks
  (VIDEO-METHODS lesson 15). From 3:7 in fig leaves: chest up, still in place. From 3:21 in skins:
  full figures may walk, which is what makes the exile shot possible. Every LTX shot checked at
  full size.
- **D3, the cherubim.** v1 showed only the sword of flame (its D5). Proposed for v2: vast wings
  of flame and shadow around the sword, never a face, a body or a human shape, and never the banned
  word in a prompt (prompts say "great wings made of fire"). The woven cherubim on the veil appear
  as embroidery. **Fallback:** the flame alone, as v1.
- **D4, the serpent.** A real snake, larger and closer than v1 (scales, eye, coils along a branch),
  never with legs, never with a human face, no speaking mouth; its words are the READER's voice
  over the snake still or moving. On screen as a creature, per the study's main view.
- **D5, the serpent turned to dust at the tomb.** A pictured image of Genesis 3:14 and 3:15 meeting
  at the resurrection (the food of the serpent will be dust). It is symbolic, not a scene the text
  narrates, so it needs your yes. **Fallback:** end the set piece on the tomb and the light.
- **D6, Christ never shown.** Gethsemane, the crown of thorns on a stone, a beam against the sky,
  the veil, the empty tomb: objects and places only, as The Garden v2.
- **D7, new GUIDE lines** (marked "new in v2" in `movement.py`, each with its study field):
  the cobra on Pharaoh's crown, "Jesus calls him", Cain and Elijah's questions, "not a sentence, a
  search", stationed and the tabernacle, Israel's exile and Hosea 6:7, Luke 3:38, the mercy seat
  and Exodus 25:22, and the open loop into Cain and Abel (the one line not from the study, flagged
  as before).
- **D8, length.** 18:47 estimated, the longest film so far, because it carries five set pieces.
  If it must come down, the first cuts are: Elijah's question (Voice in the garden), the John 8:44
  line (A crafty voice), the Hosea block (East of Eden) and the Luke 3:38 line. Together about 50 s.
