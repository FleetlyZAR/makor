# The Garden: production plan

Genesis 2:4 to 25, the second Makor movement film, after The Seven Days (uploaded). Draft
script: `script/movement-script.md` (generated by `script/movement.py`, about 10:54, 22 verses
verified). Rules, style and voices: `films/STYLE-BIBLE.md`, `films/VOICES.md`.

## What carries over from The Seven Days

- The whole pipeline: script generator (now shared, `films/tools/movement_script.py`), voice
  track, animatic, world bible, keyframes with reference images, motion, sound, edit, YouTube
  and vertical versions, the one minute short.
- **Depth motion is the standard for every still shot** (`films/tools/depth_move.py`): near
  layers move more than far ones, free, local.
- **Veo mix**: Lite for calm shots (water, grass, landscape drift), Fast for creatures, light,
  people and transitions. Each has its own daily limit (about 10 each), so both run every day.
- Clips that misbehave are trimmed to their clean seconds or replaced by a keyframe dissolve,
  never rerolled blindly.
- Fraunces for the wordmark and titles; Makor opening, corner mark, one subscribe prompt,
  20 s end screen; captions files for YouTube.

## Milestones (each ends at a checkpoint)

- **M0** decisions below, and script lock (you read the draft and mark wording).
- **M1** voice track (READER and GOD in ElevenLabs, GUIDE in Kokoro) and real timings.
- **M2** animatic.
- **M3** world bible: Eden, the rivers, the trees, the man and the woman at a distance, the
  forming from dust by effect, the animals, the deep sleep, the gardens of the passion, the
  city with the river and the tree of life.
- **M4** shot list and keyframes (batch run, reviewed as contact sheets).
- **M5** motion: Veo mix for about 30 shots, depth motion for the rest.
- **M6** sound effects and Suno score prompts (four acts: the ground and the breath; the garden;
  the command and the helper; the bride and the last Adam).
- **M7** edit, YouTube version, vertical version, one minute short, publishing packs.

## Tool work before production

1. Make the edit, platform and short tools read a per film config (act scores, sound cues,
   end card text, study address) instead of The Seven Days constants.
2. Wire depth motion into the edit for every still shot.
3. Keyframes through the Gemini Batch API at half price (results usually well within 24 hours).

## Decisions (agreed 6 October 2026)

1. **D1** The man and the woman: always at a distance, small in the landscape, from behind or
   in profile, faces never detailed, framed by trees and light so no body or clothing detail
   reads. 2:25 is two far off figures among the trees in full light. Nothing close, ever.
2. **D2** God's actions only by effect: dust drawn together by wind and light; a breath of wind
   and light; night and gathering light near the sleeping man, far off; two figures meeting in
   morning light. Never hands, never a figure.
3. **D3** The man's words in 2:23 are read by the READER (three voices in every film).
4. **D4** Eden: a lush garden in the east in the same painted world, eastern entrance, a river
   dividing into four, gold in the riverbed, the two trees apart at the centre, no serpent.
5. **D5** The helper role debate stays on the study page; the film keeps the shared ground.
6. **D6** Hard cap 75 USD, logged in `COSTS-movement.md`, checkpoint at every 25.
