# The Garden in a minute (draft for approval)

One GUIDE led short that explains the whole movement, Genesis 2:4 to 25, for Instagram
Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and WhatsApp Status. 9:16, 1080x1920, about 59.7 s (under the
60 s that suits all four; WhatsApp Status allows up to 90 s).

Intention (from study.oneStory): chapter one gave the world its shape; chapter two gives it a
centre. One idea only: **God makes a home with His people: He forms man from dust and breathes
life into him, names the one thing that is not good, and builds the woman; the Bible ends with
that home restored.**

Voices follow films/VOICES.md: READER for Scripture (including the man's words in 2:23, D3),
GOD for God's own words, GUIDE (`am_michael`) for everything else. Scripture is verbatim BSB
from the study JSON and every verse used is complete. GUIDE lines are drawn from the study
(field in brackets). Timings are measured: the Scripture lines are the film's own renders
(m010, m036, m037, m049), the GUIDE lines were rendered locally with Kokoro to time them.

Every line is captioned on screen: Scripture with its reference in gold, GUIDE in italics
without a label, as in the full film and The Seven Days short.

Picture rules checked against the film: no figure before 2:7; the woman appears only from
2:23 (s064 follows s060 in the film); every person is tiny, far off and faceless; God only by
effect (dust, wind, light); no serpent; the city shot has no sun disc.

## How it will be made (all free)

1. Voices: the film's READER and GOD renders for 2:7, 2:18 and 2:23 (already paid for);
   the four GUIDE lines rendered locally with Kokoro into `audio/short/g1..g4.wav`.
2. Pictures: the 2K keyframes cropped to 9:16 with a slow push each, soft dissolves.
3. Music and sound: the film's own score under the voices, ducked under every line; the
   film's wind, garden and night cues where they fit.
4. Tool: `edit/short.py`, adapted from The Seven Days' `edit/short.py`.
5. Outputs: `exports/the-garden-in-a-minute.mp4` (1080x1920, -14 LUFS) and
   `exports/the-garden-in-a-minute-whatsapp.mp4` (720x1280), plus `exports/short-pack.md`.
