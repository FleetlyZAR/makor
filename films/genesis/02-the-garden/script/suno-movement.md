# The Garden: Suno prompts for the score

**Not used (6 October 2026):** Luyanda chose to reuse The Seven Days score instead, re-cut
to The Garden's acts (see `music_note` in `film.json`). Kept here in case a new score is wanted later.

Four instrumental tracks, one per act, in the same family as The Seven Days score so the
two films sound like one series. Times come from the rendered voice track. Aim for the
length shown or a little longer; I cut and crossfade in the edit.

Drop the finished WAVs into `films/genesis/02-the-garden/audio/music/` (any names are fine;
I will rename them `act1.wav` to `act4.wav`). WAV is preferred.

For every track:

- Mode: Custom. Instrumental: on.
- If your Suno has "Cover" or "Use as inspiration", give it one of The Seven Days tracks
  (act 2, "Forming", fits best) so the instruments and room match.
- Exclude styles (if the box exists):

```
drums, percussion, vocals, choir, chanting, epic trailer hits, electronic beat, synth arpeggio, guitar
```

The voice carries the film: keep the speech range open, long slow arcs, no busy melodies.

---

## Act 1: The ground and the breath (0:00 to 2:19, aim 2:30)

Cold open, From the heavens to the ground, Dust and breath.

Title: `Makor The Garden Act 1 Dust and Breath`

Style:
```
intimate cinematic ambient score, solo cello and warm low strings, soft felt piano, gentle wind textures, earthy and close, very slow, a held breath of stillness that opens into a warm tender rise as life begins, no drums, no vocals
```

Lyrics box:
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro]
[descending from high airy strings to a low warm note, coming down to the ground]
[Verse]
[dry and spare, soft piano over a low drone, waiting]
[Build]
[slow tender rise, strings gather]
[Swell]
[a warm breath of life, gentle major bloom, intimate never loud]
[Outro]
[settles, calm and alive]
[End]
```
