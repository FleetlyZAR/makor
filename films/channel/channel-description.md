# YouTube channel description

Paste the block below into YouTube Studio, Customisation, Profile, Description
(limit 1,000 characters; this is about 900).

```
Cinematic Bible stories, told word for word from Scripture and filmed like a movie. Makor walks through the Bible one movement at a time, starting with the book of Genesis: Creation and the seven days, Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden, the Fall, Cain and Abel, Noah and the flood, the Tower of Babel, then Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph.

Every film is a Bible reading and a Bible study in one. You hear the text itself, see its world brought to life, and learn what it meant to its first readers and how each passage points to Jesus Christ. Faithful to the Word of God, careful with the history of the Bible, made for anyone, whether you have read Scripture for years or are opening it for the first time.

Each film has a free, in depth study to go with it at makor.co.za, with the full passage, background and questions for personal devotion or small groups.

Subscribe to follow the Bible story from Genesis onward, one book at a time.
```
