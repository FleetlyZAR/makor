Scripture of the day

Then Jesus told His disciples, “If anyone wants to come after Me, he must deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me.

Matthew 16:24

Peter has just tried to talk Jesus out of the cross, and Jesus answers by handing a cross to everyone who would follow Him.

At Caesarea Philippi Peter had confessed Him as the Christ, and Jesus at once began to teach that the Christ must suffer, die, and rise. Peter wanted the glory without the suffering. So Jesus turns to all His disciples and names the cost: to come after Him is to say no to yourself, take up a cross, and walk where He walks. In that world a man carrying a cross was on his way to die. Yet His next words hold a promise: whoever loses his life for His sake will find it. The road is hard, and He is on it ahead of us.

Lent is a good time to ask what you are still holding back. Lay it down today, and follow Him.
