Scripture of the day

Your house and kingdom will endure forever before Me, and your throne will be established forever.”

2 Samuel 7:16

David wants to build God a house, and God answers that He will build David one instead.

The promise reaches past David's lifetime: his house, his kingdom, his throne, all made secure forever. David has given nothing and yet receives everything, because the covenant rests on what God freely says He will do, not on what David can do for Him. Israel built its hope on these words for a thousand years. The New Testament sees them fulfilled in Jesus, the Son of David, whose kingdom has no end.

You do not hold up God's promises. He holds them up. Rest in that today.
