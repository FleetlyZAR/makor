Scripture of the day

“I am the Lord’s servant,” Mary answered. “May it happen to me according to your word.” Then the angel left her.

Luke 1:38

A teenager in Nazareth has just been told she will bear the King, and the whole of her reply fits in a single breath.

Mary calls herself the Lord's servant, a word for someone who belongs to her master and takes her orders from him. She does not demand a sign or bargain over what it will cost her. She asks only that God's word be done to her, as He has said it. Then the angel leaves, and she is alone with a promise she cannot yet see. God stoops to a lowly girl, and her humble faith answers Him.

Most of our yes to God is spoken before the angel leaves, when nothing is yet visible. Say it again today.
