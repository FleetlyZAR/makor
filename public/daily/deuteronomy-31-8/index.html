Scripture of the day

The LORD Himself goes before you; He will be with you. He will never leave you nor forsake you. Do not be afraid or discouraged."

Deuteronomy 31:8

Moses is a hundred and twenty years old, shut out of the land, and he is handing his people to a younger man.

He tells Joshua what he himself could not carry across the Jordan: the LORD will go ahead of him. God leads, God stays, and God does not walk away. Moses does not promise that the way will be easy; the Canaanite cities are still there. He promises Someone. That is why fear and discouragement can be put down. Joshua's courage is borrowed from the One who goes first, and the same promise is echoed to every believer in Hebrews 13.

Wherever you are being sent today, remember who has already gone ahead. You will not arrive alone.
