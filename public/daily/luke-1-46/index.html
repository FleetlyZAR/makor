Scripture of the day

Then Mary said: “My soul magnifies the Lord, and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior!

Luke 1:46-47

Elizabeth has just blessed her, and Mary answers not with a speech but with a song that has been waiting in her.

Mary begins by saying that her whole inner life, soul and spirit together, is lifted up toward God. She does not praise herself for being chosen. She calls God her Savior, which places her among the people who need saving. The song that follows celebrates a God who scatters the proud and lifts the lowly, and who keeps His promise to Abraham. Her joy rests on who He is, not on how her circumstances look.

Let gladness in God be the first thing you reach for this morning, before the list and before the news.
