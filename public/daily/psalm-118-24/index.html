Scripture of the day

This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24

The singer has come through the narrow place, near death and surrounded, and now he walks up to the temple gates with a day he can hardly believe.

The psalm has just spoken of the stone the builders threw away becoming the cornerstone, and this verse is the response. The day is not praised because it was easy or because we earned it. It is the LORD's own doing, handed over as a gift. Christians have long heard Christ in the rejected stone, and so the rejoicing here has a deeper floor than a good mood. The verse speaks in the plural: we will rejoice. It is a song for the gathered people as much as for one heart.

Before the first message arrives, receive this day as given. Gladness can begin with simply saying whose it is.
