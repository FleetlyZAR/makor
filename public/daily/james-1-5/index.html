Scripture of the day

Now if any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to him.

James 1:5

James writes to scattered believers who are under pressure, and he does not tell them to find a way out; he tells them to ask.

Wisdom here is not cleverness but the skill to live well under trial, and it is a gift. God gives it generously, to all, and without finding fault. He does not scold us for needing help or keep a list of earlier failures. James adds that we should ask in faith, not like a wave tossed by the wind. The promise is plain and the Giver is good, since every good gift comes down from the Father of lights.

If you do not know what to do today, say so to God. He is glad to be asked.
