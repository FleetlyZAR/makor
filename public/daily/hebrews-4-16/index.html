Scripture of the day

Let us then approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need.

Hebrews 4:16

For a people who knew the temple veil and the trembling of the high priest once a year, this is an astonishing invitation.

The writer has just shown us Jesus, the great high priest who has passed through the heavens and yet was tempted in every way we are, without sin. He does not watch our weakness from a distance; He feels it alongside us. So the throne of God, which we might expect to be a seat of judgment, has become a throne of grace. The word for confidence means freedom of speech: we come not cowering but speaking plainly, as welcome children. There we receive mercy for what we have done, and grace timed for the hour we are in.

Whatever you carry into this morning, take it to Him. The way to the throne is open, and He already understands.
