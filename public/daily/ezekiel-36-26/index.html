Scripture of the day

I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you; I will remove your heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh.

Ezekiel 36:26

Ezekiel spoke these words to exiles in Babylon, a people whose city lay in ruins because their hearts had turned hard toward God.

The LORD does not tell them to try harder. He promises to do the work Himself, and not because they had earned it: for the sake of His own holy name. A heart of stone cannot feel, cannot respond, cannot love; no amount of effort softens it. So God says He will take it out and give a living heart in its place, and in the next breath He promises His own Spirit within them. Jesus pointed Nicodemus back to this promise when He spoke of being born again, and it is fulfilled in the new covenant sealed by His blood.

Repentance is not making yourself new. It is bringing God the hard places and asking Him to do what only He can.
