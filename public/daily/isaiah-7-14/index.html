Scripture of the day

Therefore the Lord Himself will give you a sign: Behold, the virgin will be with child and will give birth to a son, and will call Him Immanuel.

Isaiah 7:14

King Ahaz is frightened, faithless, and too proud to ask God for anything, so God gives him a sign anyway.

The sign is a child, born of a virgin, whose name is Immanuel: God with us. In Ahaz's own day it speaks of deliverance from two threatening kings, yet it opens toward something far greater. Matthew sees its full fulfilment in Jesus. God does not hold back His promise because a king will not ask. He gives the sign, and the sign is Himself, drawing near.

You do not have to be good at asking for God to be good at giving. Receive the gift He has already sent.
