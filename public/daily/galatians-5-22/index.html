Scripture of the day

But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. Against such things there is no law.

Galatians 5:22-23

Paul has just listed the works of the flesh, a long and ugly catalogue, and now he turns to a single, quiet word: fruit.

Notice that it is fruit and not a list of achievements. Fruit grows. A branch does not strain to produce it; it stays joined to the vine and the life flows through. The nine qualities Paul names, from love to self-control, describe the character of Christ forming in those who belong to Him and walk in step with the Spirit. Paul ends with a dry, almost smiling remark: against such things there is no law. No one has ever needed a rule to forbid kindness.

You cannot force fruit into season. Stay close to Christ today, and let the Spirit grow what He will.
