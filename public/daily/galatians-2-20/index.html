Scripture of the day

I have been crucified with Christ, and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself up for me.

Galatians 2:20

Paul says it as a man who once tried to earn God's favour by keeping the law, and who gave that up for something stranger.

He has been crucified with Christ, so the old self that depended on its own record is dead. Yet Paul is alive, because Christ lives in him. His days are still lived in a body, but now by faith in the Son of God. Then he gets personal: this Son loved me and gave Himself for me. Paul does not speak of love in general. He points to the cross and says that it was for him, which is the whole ground of his new life.

Say it for yourself today, plainly: He loved me and gave Himself for me. Then live from that.
