Scripture of the day

And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed. He will crush your head, and you will strike his heel.”

Genesis 3:15

Even as the first sin is being judged, God speaks a promise into the wreckage.

The serpent has won a round, and the woman and her husband are hiding. God does not abandon them. He announces war between the serpent and the woman, and between his offspring and hers. One of her descendants will strike the serpent's head, though he will be wounded in the heel. The church has long heard in these words the first whisper of the gospel: a Son who would be hurt, yet would crush evil at its root.

Advent begins here, in a garden, with a promise. Whatever has been broken, God had a plan for it before the ground cooled.
