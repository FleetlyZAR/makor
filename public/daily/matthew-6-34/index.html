Scripture of the day

Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Today has enough trouble of its own.

Matthew 6:34

Jesus says this on a hillside to people who had real reasons to worry: taxes, hunger, and the next harvest.

He does not tell them their troubles are imaginary. He says each day carries enough of its own, and tomorrow's weight should not be borrowed in advance. His argument rests on the Father, who feeds the birds and clothes the grass, and who knows what His children need. So the call is to seek His kingdom first and to leave the rest in the hands of the One who sees. Worry adds nothing to a day; trust gives the day back to Him, one portion at a time.

Carry only today's load. Hand tomorrow to your Father, and He will meet you there.
