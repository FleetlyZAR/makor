Scripture of the day

But when the time had fully come, God sent His Son, born of a woman, born under the law, to redeem those under the law, that we might receive our adoption as sons.

Galatians 4:4-5

Paul writes of an exact moment, when the clock of history reached the hour God had chosen and sent His Son.

The Son was born of a woman, so He was truly human, and born under the law, so He stood where we stood. He came to buy back those held under the law. The purpose goes beyond rescue: we are brought into the family as sons, with the full standing of an heir. For Paul this is the opposite of slavery. God's timing was not late; it was complete.

You are not a servant waiting to be noticed. You are a child who has been adopted, and Christmas is where that began.
